## Stage I – 2025 { #stage-1 }

**Implementation of the first support activities** · July – December 2025 ·
All planned objectives were fully achieved.

### Student scholarship programme

In September 2025 a call was launched for 4 research scholarships (October 2025 – September 2026),
each supervised by DELTA-Hub researchers. Five applications were received by the 24 September
deadline; a committee of project researchers selected the scholarship holders on 24–26 September
based on CV, motivation letter and a research proposal.

| Scholarship | Research topic | Holder |
|---|---|---|
| Hydro-sedimentary modelling | Integrating Lidar-derived topography and sediment transport in a digital-twin framework for assessing human impacts on the Danube Delta | Răzvan-Laurențiu Dogaru |
| Hydro-ecological modelling | How variations in hydrological regimes, such as droughts and floods, affect vegetation growth, habitat suitability and biodiversity in the Danube Delta | Otilia Andreea Cotelin |
| 3D visualisation and VR | Quantifying the dynamics of the Sfântu Gheorghe distributary mouth using modern visualisation and analysis methods | Andreea Miron |
| Climate analysis | Potential shifts in wind regimes and storminess in the Western Black Sea and their influence on wave dynamics | Andrei Șelăvîrtea |

The scholarship holders started on 1 October 2025; preliminary results will be presented to the
DELTA-Hub researchers in the first quarter of 2026.

### Project website

A project web page was created within the ERA Chairs DELTA-Hub website
([delta-hub.unibuc.ro/visualization.html](https://delta-hub.unibuc.ro/visualization.html)), with general
information, reports and related information. It will also host the laboratory's dissemination
products: 3D models, VR animations and presentation films.

### Mobile component of the visualisation laboratory

Equipment acquired for the DELTA-Hub Visualisation and Science Outreach Laboratory:

- uncrewed surface vehicle (USV) for autonomous bathymetric surveys in shallow areas of the Danube Delta (lakes, channels, marshes);
- drone for aerial filming;
- 360° video camera and compact action camera for field campaigns;
- VR headsets for presenting virtual-reality models and immersive materials to the public;
- portable generator and high-capacity battery for extended autonomy during field surveys;
- laser projector with stand and projection screen for outreach events;
- workstation with two monitors for data processing, complex simulations, numerical models and visualisation.

These activities lay the groundwork for the full operation of the laboratory and for the first
virtual models of the Danube Delta, used in dissemination activities in 2026.
