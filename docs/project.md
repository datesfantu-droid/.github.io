# About the project

## Context

Since {{ project.related_project.start }}, the University of Bucharest (UB), through its
Research Institute (ICUB), hosts **{{ project.related_project.name }}**, funded by
{{ project.related_project.programme }}. DELTA-Hub aims to turn UB into a European centre of
excellence for the research, modelling, and education of river deltas, with a focus on the
Danube Delta and global relevance, especially for the Black Sea and Mediterranean basins.

DELTA-Hub has four interconnected components:

1. **Science** – advanced technologies: remote sensing, numerical modelling, field measurements;
2. **Education** – a Master's programme in coastal and marine sciences applied to delta management;
3. **Institutional capacity building** at UB;
4. **Engagement** through the transfer of scientific knowledge.

This project supports and consolidates DELTA-Hub through activities aligned with the UB
Development Strategy 2024–2028 (Research Sector Strategy OS1–OS3 and Strategic Plan OS1–OS2).

## Specific objectives

### OS1 – Institutional capacity and human resources

- **8 Master's scholarships** (400 EUR/month, 1 year each) for students in geosciences or technical
  fields (hydraulic engineering, computer science);
- **3 performance awards** (1,000 EUR over 3 months) for the best students in workshops and hackathons;
- **2 postdoctoral fellowships** (800 EUR/month, 1 year each) for young researchers who publish
  results and mentor students;
- **1 technical position** for bathymetric surveys and equipment maintenance.

Students contribute 3D models for VR experiences, numerical modelling and satellite image analysis.
All positions are filled through open, merit-based selection that respects equal opportunities and
gender equality.

### OS2 – Internationalisation and research partnerships

- **International mobilities** – at least 6 mobilities of 7 days each, for 5 people per mobility, to
  institutions of the International Advisory Board (IAB);
- **Annual IAB strategic meetings** in the Danube Delta (years II and III), combining strategic
  discussions, field work and proposal-writing sessions for European funding (Horizon Europe, ERC);
- **Collaboration with the private sector** for knowledge transfer and training.

### OS3 – DELTA-Hub Visualisation and Science Outreach Laboratory

- **Fixed component** at the Sf. Gheorghe Marine and Fluvial Research Station (SCMF), Danube Delta:
  high-performance workstations and monitors for data processing, simulations and visualisation;
- **Mobile component**: laptops, VR headsets (WebXR-compatible) and a high-brightness laser
  projector for Open Days, European Researchers' Night and other outreach events;
- **Web platform** linked to this website, accessible from any device (including VR headsets), with
  high-resolution bathymetric and topographic 3D models of the Danube Delta, hydrodynamic and
  morphodynamic simulation animations, drone and FPV footage, and 360° field imagery.

## Research equipment

1. **Shallow-water bathymetry system (0–4 m)** – maps critical areas such as river-mouth bars,
   beaches and shallow lakes that existing equipment cannot reach;
2. **SBG IMU-GNSS system** – an inertial measurement unit combined with satellite navigation,
   integrated with the existing multibeam system to correct wave-induced vessel motion.

Together they improve data quality and the accuracy of 3D models used for research, delta-evolution
predictions, and visualisation.

## Expected results

- 11 student scholarships/awards, 2 postdoctoral fellowships and 1 technical position;
- at least 6 international mobilities and 2 IAB strategic meetings;
- at least 1 targeted European project proposal;
- a fixed and a mobile VR visualisation laboratory and an associated web platform;
- high-resolution bathymetric datasets and 3D models of the Danube Delta;
- scientific publications and outreach events.
