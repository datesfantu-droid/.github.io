# Results & Impact

## Main results

<!-- Update after each report. -->

- **2025:** 4 research scholarships awarded (hydro-sedimentary modelling, hydro-ecological modelling,
  3D visualisation/VR, climate analysis); project web page launched; mobile component of the
  visualisation laboratory equipped (USV for bathymetry, drone, 360° camera, VR headsets, laser
  projector, workstation). See [Reports](reports.md).

## Scientific (cognitive) impact

- High-resolution bathymetric data for previously unmapped shallow areas of the Danube Delta
  (river-mouth bars, beaches, shallow lakes), improving numerical models of delta evolution;
- Young researchers trained in remote sensing, numerical modelling and field measurements;
- Stronger international collaboration through the IAB and more European funding proposals.

## Socio-economic impact

- Knowledge transfer to public authorities and the private sector on coastal and delta management;
- Public engagement through immersive VR experiences at Open Days, European Researchers' Night and
  other events, attracting young people to science;
- Research infrastructure at SCMF Sf. Gheorghe available to researchers and students.
